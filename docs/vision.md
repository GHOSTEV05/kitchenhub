# Project Vision

## Purpose

KitchenHub is a SaaS platform designed to help restaurants optimize their daily operations through the digitalization and automation of administrative processes.

The system aims to improve operational efficiency, reduce the time spent on manual tasks, and provide a better experience for both restaurant staff and customers.

## Problem Statement

Restaurants that lack specialized management systems often experience lower operational efficiency and higher operating costs.

Manual order registration increases the likelihood of inconsistencies and human errors, extends customer waiting times, and requires employees to spend valuable time on administrative tasks that could otherwise be automated. As a result, both staff productivity and customer experience are negatively affected.

Additionally, manually managing inventory may lead to waste, product losses, unnecessary purchases, and inaccurate stock records. Manual cash management and sales tracking can also result in accounting errors, while the absence of reliable operational data makes informed decision-making more difficult.

## Proposed Solution

KitchenHub provides a centralized platform that enables restaurants to efficiently manage their most important operational processes from a single system.

By automating routine tasks, maintaining real-time business data, and providing valuable operational insights, the platform aims to improve staff productivity, reduce operational errors, and simplify the day-to-day management of restaurant operations.

## Objectives

### General Objective

Develop a solution that automates and improves the administrative management of restaurant operations.

### Specific Objectives

- Design a scalable and maintainable system architecture that supports the restaurant's core business processes.
- Implement the functionalities defined for the Minimum Viable Product (MVP), including user authentication, menu management, order management, and inventory management.
- Develop a secure and reliable backend API to support communication between the user interface and the business logic.
- Ensure the quality, security, and reliability of the system through testing and validation.

## Target Users

- Customers
- Administrative staff
- Cashiers
- Waiters
- Cooks

## Scope

### In Scope

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of KitchenHub will include the following modules:

- User authentication and role-based access control.
- Menu and product management.
- Order management.
- Inventory management.
- Basic financial overview.
- Basic administrative reports.

### Out of Scope

The MVP will not include features such as online payments, delivery management, third-party platform integrations, customer loyalty programs, advanced analytics, or mobile applications. These features may be considered for future releases.

## Core Features

- User authentication and role management.
- Menu and product management.
- Order management.
- Inventory management.
- Basic financial dashboard.
- Administrative reporting.

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

- The MVP features are fully implemented and functional.
- The application provides a stable and intuitive user experience.
- Core business processes can be completed without errors.
- The system follows the defined architecture and coding standards.
- The application is ready to be deployed in a production environment.

## Constraints

The project is subject to the following constraints:

- Development is carried out by a two-person team.
- Development time is limited by the academic schedule of the team members.
- The first release will focus only on the MVP.
- The system will initially support a single restaurant.
- The project will be developed using the selected technology stack.

## Assumptions

The project assumes that:

- Users have access to a stable internet connection.
- Restaurant staff have basic digital literacy.
- Business requirements may evolve during development.
- The selected technologies are sufficient to support the project's objectives.
- The restaurant operates using standardized business processes.

## Future Vision

Future versions of KitchenHub may include:

- Online payment integration.
- Delivery management.
- Integration with third-party delivery platforms.
- Multi-branch support.
- Mobile applications.
- Advanced analytics and business intelligence.
- Customer loyalty programs.
- AI-powered features for business optimization.
